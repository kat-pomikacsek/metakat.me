# Impeccable — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary audience:** Recruiters and hiring managers at startups evaluating Katalin for staff product designer roles. They're scanning quickly, looking for signals of seniority, design systems expertise, and cross-functional collaboration. They likely arrive via a LinkedIn link or resume URL and spend 2–5 minutes forming an impression.

**Secondary audience:** Design peers or engineers looking up the portfolio before interviews — assessing whether this person is someone they'd want to work with, checking depth of thinking and process.

**Job to be done:** Get a solid, quick overview of professional background and skills. Walk away understanding that Katalin is a structured designer, strong with design systems, a cross-functional collaborator, user-centered, and self-driven.

### Brand Personality

**Three words:** Structured, curious, grounded.

- **Structured:** The skills section is methodically organized, the process is end-to-end (kickoff to launch), the thinking is systematic. This isn't a vibes-first designer — it's someone who brings order to complexity.
- **Curious:** Sociology degree, multilingual, graphic novel collector, hiker, DIY maker. A breadth of interest that feeds the design work.
- **Grounded:** "Pens, paper, sticky notes" listed as tools. Practical, approachable, no pretension. The copy is warm but professional — never flashy.

**Tone of voice:** Direct and warm. First-person, conversational but professional. No jargon, no self-promotion — lets the work and experience speak.

**Emotional goal:** The visitor should feel confidence in Katalin's ability and a sense of her as a real person, not just a skill list.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Visual tone:** Clean, professional, quietly confident. Light theme with white base and warm grays (#333 body text). Orange (#FF6D12) as a focused accent — used sparingly for bullet markers, hover states, and the logo bar. Case study themes (petrol, sky, mango, peach) add variety without breaking cohesion.

**Typography:** Overpass (400–700) as the sole typeface. Responsive body text (base to xl at sm breakpoint). Negative letter-spacing on headings. H3 uppercase for section labels.

**Layout:** Content-width constrained to 720px for readability. Fixed header, container-based sections with horizontal margins on mobile. Case studies use split-background gradients and wider containers for screen mockups.

**Theme:** Light. Appropriate for the audience context — hiring managers and recruiters browsing during work hours on laptops.

**Anti-patterns to avoid:** No dark mode glow, no gradient text, no glassmorphism, no card-heavy dashboard aesthetics. This is a portfolio for a senior designer — the design itself is the proof of competence.

### Design Principles

1. **Substance over spectacle.** Every design choice should demonstrate the structured, user-centered thinking the portfolio claims. The site _is_ the work sample.
2. **Respect the scanner.** Primary users are time-constrained. Information hierarchy, scannability, and clear structure matter more than novelty.
3. **Quiet confidence.** The aesthetic should feel assured without trying hard. Let whitespace, typography, and restraint do the work.
4. **Warm professionalism.** Not cold or corporate, not casual or playful. The sweet spot is competent and approachable — like a well-run design review.
5. **Accessibility first.** WCAG AA compliance. Meaningful contrast, proper semantics, keyboard navigable, reduced-motion safe.

### Technical Constraints

- **Framework:** Astro 6 (static output)
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS 4 (theme via CSS, no config file)
- **CMS:** Contentful (case studies, images)
- **Fonts:** Google Fonts (Overpass), loaded via Astro font providers
- **Accessibility target:** WCAG AA
